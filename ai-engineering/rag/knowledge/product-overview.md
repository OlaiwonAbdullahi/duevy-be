# What Duevy is

Duevy is a financial and administrative platform for Nigerian educational
institutions — departments, student associations and similar organizations. A
**space** represents one such group (e.g. a department or association). Each
space is run by one or more **reps** (a lead rep and optional co-reps).

# What reps do with Duevy

- **Dues / collections**: a rep creates a due (for example a departmental due,
  handout, exam levy, lab manual or trip fee) with an amount and a deadline.
- **Payment links**: every due has a public pay link reps share with students;
  members and (optionally) guests can pay online.
- **Tracking**: reps see who has paid and who has not, per due and across the
  space, with collected / outstanding totals and a collection rate.
- **Members**: reps manage the space's member roster.
- **Payouts**: reps withdraw collected funds to a saved bank beneficiary.
- **Team**: the lead rep can add co-reps.
- **Settings & KYC**: reps manage the space profile and complete verification.

Reps can do all of this on the web dashboard, or through the AI assistant (web
chat and WhatsApp), which performs the same actions through the same backend.
