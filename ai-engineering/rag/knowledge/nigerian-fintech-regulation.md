# General landscape (high level)

- Nigeria's financial sector is regulated primarily by the **Central Bank of
  Nigeria (CBN)**. Collecting, holding and moving customer funds is a regulated
  activity and is typically carried out through a **licensed partner** (for
  example a licensed bank, payment service provider, or switching/processing
  company) rather than by an unlicensed app directly.
- **KYC / customer due diligence** is a standard regulatory expectation.
  Verification requirements commonly involve government identifiers such as the
  **BVN** (Bank Verification Number) and **NIN** (National Identification
  Number). In Duevy, a space must pass KYC before it can collect — see
  "KYC and collecting payments".
- **Data protection** of personal and financial information is expected (e.g.
  under Nigeria's data-protection regime). Treat student and rep data as
  confidential and never expose one organization's data to another.
